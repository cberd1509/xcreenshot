# Privacy

xcreenshot keeps your screenshots on your device.

- No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.
- Screenshots are never uploaded. They only go where you send them: the clipboard, a file you save, or the share sheet.
- The only network requests are for updates. Once a day xcreenshot downloads `latest.json` from GitHub Releases, and the update itself when there is a new version. GitHub sees your IP address and that a check happened; no other data is sent. Update checks are on for now; a switch to turn them off is coming with Settings ([#20](https://github.com/cberd1509/xcreenshot/issues/20)).
