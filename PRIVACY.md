# Privacy

xcreenshot keeps your screenshots on your device.

- No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.
- Screenshots are never uploaded. They only go where you send them: the clipboard, a file you save, or the share sheet.
- The only network requests are for updates. Once a day xcreenshot downloads `latest.json` from GitHub Releases, and the update itself when there is a new version. GitHub sees your IP address and that a check happened; no other data is sent. Automatic checks can be turned off in Settings › General › Check for updates. **Check for updates…** in the menu bar always checks when you ask.
