# Code of Conduct

xcreenshot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/cberd1509). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
